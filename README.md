# Geographical Analysis 

## Week 14 – Data Analytics Assignment

**Dataset:** Global Superstore Dataset  
**Project Type:** Geographical Data Visualization and Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing geographical sales and profit data using Tableau Public. The Global Superstore dataset is used to understand how business performance varies across different countries, states, regions, and cities.

The data is represented through interactive maps and charts to make regional comparisons easier and to identify important sales patterns.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyze sales distribution across different countries.
- Compare profit performance across states.
- Understand sales variations between geographical regions.
- Identify the top 10 cities based on total sales.
- Present the results through an interactive dashboard.

## 3. Dataset Description

The Global Superstore dataset contains business transaction details related to orders, products, customers, sales, and geographical locations.

**Important fields used:**

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Country | Country-wise sales analysis |
| State | State-wise profit analysis |
| City | City-wise sales ranking |
| Region | Regional sales comparison |
| Sales | Total sales calculation |
| Profit | Profit performance analysis |

## 4. Visualizations Created

### Sheet 1 – Global Sales Distribution by Country
A geographical map showing the distribution of total sales across different countries. Colour variations represent differences in sales performance.

### Sheet 2 – State-wise Profit and Sales Performance
A geographical map displaying state-level profit and sales information. It helps identify profitable and loss-making locations.

### Sheet 3 – Regional Sales Performance Comparison
A horizontal bar chart comparing total sales across different geographical regions. It helps identify the regions contributing the highest sales.

### Sheet 4 – Top 10 Cities by Sales Performance
A horizontal bar chart displaying the ten cities with the highest total sales, arranged in descending order.

## 5. Dashboard

All four visualizations are combined into a single dashboard to provide an overall view of geographical sales and profit performance.

The dashboard is designed to support interactive exploration through geographical and regional filters.

## 6. Key Findings

1. **Regional Performance:** The Central region recorded the highest sales, with approximately $2.82 million.

2. **City Performance:** New York City ranked first among the selected top 10 cities, generating approximately $256,368.16 in sales.

3. **Sales Distribution:** The global sales map shows differences in sales contribution across countries, highlighting variations in geographical business performance.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Focus on High-Performing Regions:** Businesses can strengthen their operations in regions with higher sales and identify opportunities to increase their market share.

2. **Improve Low-Profit Locations:** States and regions with lower or negative profits should be examined to identify possible reasons, such as high costs or low sales performance.

## 8. Conclusion

This geographical analysis provides a clear understanding of sales and profit variations across countries, states, regions, and cities. The visualizations make it easier to compare business performance and identify important geographical patterns.

The interactive Tableau dashboard brings the analysis together in one place and supports better understanding of regional business performance.
